Okan Efe Öğretmen

XR Designer-Researcher · Interaction Designer · Developer

Koç University · KARMA Mixed Reality Lab · Sarıyer, İstanbul

I’m a multidisciplinary researcher, designer, and developer working across XR, interaction design, and immersive experiences for consumer and enterprise contexts. I’m a fast learner and creative problem-solver, passionate about visual design and exploring how emerging technologies can shape future forms of interaction and everyday experiences.

 I recently completed my Ph.D. in Design, Technology and Society at Koç University. My doctoral research explored XR-mediated communication in domestic spaces, combining co-design studies, participant observation, and Unity/C# prototyping with qualitative analysis and the Continuous Fuzzy Kano Model.

I’m currently seeking academic opportunities, particularly faculty positions in XR, HCI, interaction design, and related fields. I’m also open to collaborating on research and development projects as an XR developer, interaction designer, or full-stack developer, and to visiting-researcher opportunities with labs working on XR, UX research, product design, spatial interaction, or immersive interfaces.

My research and design interests include UX research through XR, XR-enhanced product design, XR in everyday domestic environments, spatial interaction, and UI/interaction design for immersive experiences.
